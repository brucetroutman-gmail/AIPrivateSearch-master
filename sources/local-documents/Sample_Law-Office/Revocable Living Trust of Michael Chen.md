# Revocable Living Trust of Michael Chen

Revocable Living Trust of Michael Chen
I, Michael Chen, residing at 789 Pine Rd, Springfield, IL 62703, hereby establish this
Revocable Living Trust on this 21st day of October, 2025, to hold and manage my
assets during my lifetime and to distribute them upon my death, revoking any prior
trust agreements.
1 Appointment of Trustee
I appoint myself, Michael Chen, as the initial Trustee of this Trust. If I am unable or
unwilling to serve, I appoint my spouse, Linda Chen, as Successor Trustee. If Linda
Chen is unable or unwilling to serve, I appoint my brother, Steven Chen, as alternate
Successor Trustee. The Trustee shall have the authority to manage and distribute
the Trust assets in accordance with this document.
2 Trust Property
The Trust shall consist of the following assets, transferred to the Trust upon execu-
tion of this document:
• Real property located at 789 Pine Rd, Springfield, IL 62703.
• Investment account with Springfield Wealth Management, account number 789123456.
• Savings account at Springfield Bank, account number 321654987.
3 Beneficiaries
I designate the following individuals as primary beneficiaries of this Trust:
• Linda Chen, my spouse, residing at 789 Pine Rd, Springfield, IL 62703.
• Daniel Chen, my son, residing at 101 Elm St, Springfield, IL 62704.
4 Distribution of Trust Assets
During my lifetime, the Trustee shall distribute income and principal as I direct.
Upon my death, the Trustee shall distribute the Trust assets as follows:
1
• To Linda Chen, my spouse, the real property at 789 Pine Rd, Springfield, IL 62703,
and 60% of the investment account.
• To Daniel Chen, my son, 40% of the investment account and the savings account,
to be held in trust until he reaches the age of 30.
• Any remaining assets shall be distributed equally to Linda Chen and Daniel Chen.
5 Trust Administration
The Trustee shall have the power to manage, invest, and reinvest Trust assets, pay
Trust expenses, and make distributions as outlined. The Trust may be revoked or
amended by me during my lifetime by written notice to the Trustee.
6 Debts and Taxes
The Trustee shall pay all just debts, funeral expenses, and taxes from the Trust as-
sets as soon as practicable after my death.
7 Signature
I, Michael Chen, sign this Trust on this 21st day of October, 2025, in Springfield,
Illinois.
