# Revocable Living Trust of Mia Walker

Revocable Living Trust of Mia Walker
I, Mia Walker, residing at 909 Sycamore Dr, Springfield, IL 62712, hereby establish
this Revocable Living Trust on this 21st day of October, 2025, to hold and manage
my assets during my lifetime and to distribute them upon my death, revoking any
prior trust agreements.
1 Appointment of Trustee
I appoint myself, Mia Walker, as the initial Trustee of this Trust. If I am unable or
unwilling to serve, I appoint my husband, Edward Walker, as Successor Trustee. If
Edward Walker is unable or unwilling to serve, I appoint my sister, Charlotte Young,
as alternate Successor Trustee. The Trustee shall have the authority to manage and
distribute the Trust assets in accordance with this document.
2 Trust Property
The Trust shall consist of the following assets, transferred to the Trust upon execu-
tion of this document:
• Real property located at 909 Sycamore Dr, Springfield, IL 62712.
• Savings account at Springfield Credit Union, account number 654321789.
• Personal property, including my jewelry collection and family heirlooms.
3 Beneficiaries
I designate the following individuals as primary beneficiaries of this Trust:
• Edward Walker, my husband, residing at 909 Sycamore Dr, Springfield, IL 62712.
• Sophie Walker, my daughter, residing at 909 Sycamore Dr, Springfield, IL 62712.
4 Distribution of Trust Assets
During my lifetime, the Trustee shall distribute income and principal as I direct.
Upon my death, the Trustee shall distribute the Trust assets as follows:
1
• To Edward Walker, my husband, the real property at 909 Sycamore Dr, Springfield,
IL 62712, and my savings account.
• To Sophie Walker, my daughter, my jewelry collection and family heirlooms, to be
held in trust until she reaches the age of 25.
• Any remaining assets shall be divided equally between Edward Walker and So-
phie Walker.
5 Trust Administration
The Trustee shall have the power to manage, invest, and reinvest Trust assets, pay
Trust expenses, and make distributions as outlined. The Trust may be revoked or
amended by me during my lifetime by written notice to the Trustee.
6 Debts and Taxes
The Trustee shall pay all just debts, funeral expenses, and taxes from the Trust as-
sets as soon as practicable after my death.
7 Signature
I, Mia Walker, sign this Trust on this 21st day of October, 2025, in Springfield, Illinois.
