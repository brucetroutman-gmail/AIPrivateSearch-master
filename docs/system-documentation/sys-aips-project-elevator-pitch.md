## 30 Seconds

AIPrivateSearch is a private AI document search tool that runs entirely on your own computer. You can search through your documents using plain English, and the AI never sends your files anywhere — everything stays on your machine. It's designed for doctors, lawyers, and anyone who needs powerful AI search without the privacy risk of cloud services.

---

## 60 Seconds

AIPrivateSearch lets you search your private documents using AI — and unlike tools like ChatGPT or Google, your files never leave your computer.

You get seven different ways to search: from simple exact-text matching to AI-powered semantic search that understands what you're asking, even if you don't use the exact words in the document. You can also score AI responses automatically to measure quality.

It's built for professionals who handle sensitive information — medical practices, law firms, consultants — but it works just as well for personal document management.

There's no subscription to an AI service, no per-query fees, and no data going to the cloud. The AI runs locally using open-source models through a tool called Ollama.

We offer three tiers starting at $49 a year, with a 60-day free trial.
